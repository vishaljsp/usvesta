How We Use Cookies

At US VESTA, we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and provide a smoother, more personalized interaction with our website.

A cookie is a small file that asks permission to be placed on your computer’s hard drive. Once you agree, the file is added, and the cookie helps analyze web traffic or notifies you when you visit a particular site. Cookies allow web applications to respond to you as an individual — tailoring operations based on your needs, preferences, and behavior.

1. Purpose of Cookies

We use cookies primarily to:

  • Identify which pages are being visited and how visitors interact with them.
  • Analyze data about webpage traffic to help improve our website’s structure, design, and functionality.
  • Personalize your online experience by remembering your preferences, login details, and browsing behavior.
  • Provide relevant recommendations and promotional content based on your interests.

The information collected through cookies is used strictly for statistical and analytical purposes and is automatically removed from our system after processing.

2. Benefits of Cookies

Overall, cookies help us offer a better, more user-friendly website by:

  • Monitoring which pages you find useful and which you don’t.
  • Improving navigation speed and performance.
  • Saving you from re-entering information multiple times during a session.

Please note that a cookie in no way gives us access to your computer or any personal information other than the data you choose to share with us.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can choose to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline them if you prefer. However, doing so may prevent you from taking full advantage of our website’s features.

If you wish to disable cookies, you can usually find this option under your browser’s “Privacy” or “Settings” menu.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some of our website pages may include cookies from trusted third-party services such as Google Analytics, which help us analyze visitor behavior and improve user experience. These cookies are governed by the third-party providers’ own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Consent

By continuing to browse or use our website www.usvesta.com, you agree to our use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy.

Your privacy matters to us.
