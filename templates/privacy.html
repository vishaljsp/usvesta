How We Use Cookies

A cookie is a small file that asks permission to be placed on your computer’s hard drive. Once you agree, the cookie helps analyze web traffic or identifies when you visit a specific site. Cookies allow web applications to respond to you as an individual — customizing operations based on your preferences.

We use traffic log cookies to analyze which pages are being used. This helps us improve our website and tailor it to customer needs. The data is used for statistical analysis only and is then deleted from the system.

Overall, cookies help us provide you with a better browsing experience. Cookies do not give us access to your computer or personal data other than what you choose to share.

You can choose to accept or decline cookies. Most browsers accept cookies automatically, but you can modify your browser settings to decline them if you prefer. Doing so may limit your experience on the website.

  • If any information we hold about you is incorrect, please contact us for prompt correction.
