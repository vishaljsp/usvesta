Shipping and Delivery Polic

For the purpose of this Shipping and Delivery Policy, the terms “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to US VESTA, a sole proprietorship company, and “you” refers to the customer or buyer purchasing from our website www.usvesta.com.

Shipping for Domestic Orders

For domestic buyers, all orders are shipped through registered domestic courier companies and/or India Post Speed Post services.

  • Orders are usually shipped within 0–7 business days or as per the schedule agreed upon at the time of confirmation.
  • Delivery timelines depend on your location and the courier company’s service availability.
  • We ensure timely dispatch, but US VESTA is not liable for delays caused by courier partners, postal authorities, weather, or unforeseen issues.
  • We guarantee to hand over consignments to courier/postal authorities within 0–7 days from successful payment or as per agreed delivery terms.
Shipping for International Orders

For international buyers, all orders are shipped and delivered through registered international courier companies and/or International Speed Post only.

  • Delivery times may vary based on destination and customs clearance procedures.
  • Any additional custom duties, import taxes, or charges levied by the destination country will be borne by the buyer.
Delivery Address

All deliveries will be made to the shipping address provided by the buyer at checkout.

  • Please ensure that delivery details (name, address, pin code, phone number, etc.) are accurate and complete.
  • US VESTA shall not be responsible for delivery delays or losses due to incorrect or incomplete address details.
